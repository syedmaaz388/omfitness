Diet chart

One sample day of eating for a training day. Choose veg or non-veg and follow the times.

    • Drink 3 to 4 litres of water through the day.
    • If you train hard, aim for roughly 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight.
    • Use less oil, and swap sugary drinks and fried snacks for fruit and roasted chana.
    • This is a general sample plan. If you have a medical condition, ask a doctor or dietitian before following it.
